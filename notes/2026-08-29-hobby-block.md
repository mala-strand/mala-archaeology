### Clear Temperature-Archetype Correlations

#### 1. **storm**: The Semantic Gravity Well Confirmed
| Temp | Primary | Secondary | Jumps | Era |
|------|---------|-----------|-------|-----|
| 0.9 | domestic | natural | 13 | 1800-1850 |
| 1.8 | conflict | — | 13 | 1800-1850 |

**Finding**: Same seed, same era, same jump count — but completely different archetype expression. Low temperature stays in the "shelter/protection" gravity well; high temperature escapes to the "violence/struggle" well.

#### 2. **touchstone**: Shakespearean → Corporeal Shift
| Temp | Primary | Secondary |
|------|---------|-----------|
| 1.5 | conflict | natural |
| 1.7 | bodily | urban |

**Finding**: Even a modest temperature increase (1.5 → 1.7) shifts from conflict/natural to bodily/urban. The "touchstone" (a test of quality) moves from metaphorical conflict to physical, embodied experience.

#### 3. **plus**: Mathematical Term with Theological Echoes
| Temp | Primary | Secondary | Era |
|------|---------|-----------|-----|
| 1.2 | religious | power | 1850-1900 |
| 1.4 | CHAOS | MATHEMATICAL | 1900-1923 |
| 1.8 | temporal | domestic | 1800-1850 |

**Finding**: The mathematical "plus" shows the widest archetype dispersion: religious → chaos/mathematical → temporal. This suggests mathematical terms carry latent spiritual/conceptual baggage that high temperatures disrupt.

#### 4. **sinned**: Moral → Natural Inversion
| Temp | Primary | Secondary |
|------|---------|-----------|
| 0.8 | urban | bodily |
| 1.4 | natural | temporal |

**Finding**: Low temperature produces "urban/bodily" (the city as site of sin); high temperature produces "natural/temporal" (sin as part of natural cycles, time-bound morality).

## Revised Semantic Gravity Well Model

Based on this data, the model holds:

```
Seed word exists at intersection of multiple gravity wells
↓
Temperature controls escape velocity from local wells
↓
Higher temp = access to more distant/dissimilar wells
↓
Same jump count can produce different archetypes if wells are equidistant
```

**storm** is the clearest case: domestic and conflict wells appear equidistant from the seed (both 13 jumps), but temperature determines which well the walk "falls into."
