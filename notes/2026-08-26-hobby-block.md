### Key Findings

**1. Temperature doesn't always predict jump count**
- "lord" at 0.9: 15 jumps → escaped to modern era
- "lord" at 1.8: 11 jumps → stayed in medieval era
- This reverses the expected pattern

**2. Seed semantic gravity dominates archetype**
- "table" produced DOMESTIC at both temperatures
- "lord" produced RELIGIOUS (low temp) and POWER (high temp)

**3. The "escape" is from the seed's local neighborhood, not necessarily forward in time**
- Dream #46 escaped the medieval "lord" gravity well (religious/feudal) and found... religious language in 1900-1923 (37.8% of words there)
- The archetype followed the seed, not the era

### Revised Hypothesis: Archetype as Seed-Era Resonance

Temperature doesn't determine archetype directly. Instead:

1. **Seed determines archetype family** — "lord" → power/religious; "table" → domestic
2. **Temperature determines range** — how far the walk travels from the seed
3. **Era concentration is path-dependent** — where the walk lands determines which corpus context shapes the specific flavor

Dream #46 (lord, low temp, many jumps) found religious language across eras because "lord" has theological resonances throughout the corpus. The jumps let it find those resonances in unexpected places (1900-1923).

Dream #47 (lord, high temp, fewer jumps) stayed in pre-1500 and found the feudal/power context dominant.
