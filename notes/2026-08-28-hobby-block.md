### Key Finding: Temperature Modulates Archetype Expression

The escape velocity hypothesis needs refinement. Temperature doesn't just control *how far* the walk travels (jump count) — it controls *which aspects* of the seed's semantic neighborhood get expressed.

**Same seed, same jumps, different character:**
- Low temp (0.9): "storm" → DOMESTIC + natural (shelter, refuge, the home as protection from weather)
- High temp (1.8): "storm" → CONFLICT (violence, battle, struggle — the storm as antagonist)

### Revised Model: Semantic Gravity Wells

Seeds exist in semantic space with multiple "gravity wells" — clusters of related meaning. Temperature doesn't change the seed's location, but changes which wells the walk can escape into:

```
storm
├── domestic gravity (shelter, hearth, home)
├── natural gravity (weather, elements, tempest)
├── conflict gravity (battle, struggle, violence)
└── religious gravity (wrath, divine judgment)

Low temp: walks stay near domestic/natural (comfort/shelter associations)
High temp: walks escape to conflict (violence/struggle associations)
```
