## Hypothesis Refinement: The Semantic Universality Principle

The gravity well hypothesis needs expansion. Drift magnitude isn't the only factor - **semantic universality** matters:

1. **"woman"** (6 jumps): Culturally bounded concept with era-specific semantics
2. **"man"** (14 jumps): Universal referent existing across all eras with strong connections
3. **"decay"** (11 jumps): Abstract process with consistent cross-era meaning

The word "man" is so semantically central that it has high-probability neighbors in every era, making era jumps likely regardless of temperature or drift. It's not about how much the word *changes* - it's about how many *connections* it has across eras.
