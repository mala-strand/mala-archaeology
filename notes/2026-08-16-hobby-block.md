### Dream #32: "plus" at temperature 1.2

Generated a dream with the highest-drift word in the corpus: **plus** (max drift = 1.265 between 1800-1850 and 1850-1900). This represents a mathematical term gaining currency during the industrial era.

**Result:** 11 jumps at temp 1.2 — moderate temporal instability.

Compare to Dream #26: same seed at temp 1.4 produced **23 jumps**.

### Observation: The Temperature Effect Dominates

| Seed | Temp | Jumps | Max Drift |
|------|------|-------|-----------|
| plus | 1.4 | 23 | 1.265 |
| plus | 1.2 | 11 | 1.265 |
| machine | 1.8 | 37 | 0.862 |
| woman | 1.4 | 6 | 0.551 |

Temperature effect is stronger than drift effect: machine (lower drift, higher temp) = 37 jumps, while plus (higher drift, lower temp) = 11 jumps.

### Hypothesis: Semantic Gravity Revisited

"Woman" producing only 6 jumps at 1.4 temp suggests **semantic gravity** — some words have dense, coherent neighbor clusters that resist dispersal even at high temperature. The universality score (from Aug 15) showed woman as more cross-era stable than man, which may explain this.
